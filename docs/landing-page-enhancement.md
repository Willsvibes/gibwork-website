# Landing Page Enhancement Summary

## Research Notes

Reviewed the current Gibwork landing page, live app, iOS listing, Android listing, docs, and legal pages to compare the public marketing message with the current product experience.

Key references:

- Live landing page: https://gib.work/
- Gibwork app: https://app.gib.work/
- iOS app listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gibwork/id6757281508
- Android app listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gibwork.app
- Gibwork docs: https://docs.gib.work/info
- Terms page: https://app.gib.work/terms

## Issues Identified

1. The hero positioning was too generic.

   The previous headline, "Find Talent, Find Work", described a broad freelance marketplace but did not clearly communicate Gibwork's current onchain task, bounty, submission, and payout experience.

2. Mobile app support was missing from the landing page story.

   The current iOS and Android listings highlight browsing opportunities, submitting completed work, tracking submissions, and managing earnings from mobile. The landing page did not surface this product expansion strongly enough.

3. The requester and contributor workflows needed clearer framing.

   The old page mentioned help and work, but did not strongly connect the flow from funded task creation to contributor discovery, submission review, approval, and wallet payout.

4. Trust and product copy felt outdated.

   The partner section used broad company language instead of explaining why Solana teams and crypto-native builders use Gibwork: turning GitHub issues, content work, QA, research, and services into paid opportunities.

5. FAQ, metadata, and app links lagged behind the product.

   SEO metadata, FAQ answers, navigation, footer links, and CTA copy still focused on a general "find talent/find work" pitch. They did not clearly mention mobile apps, stablecoin payouts, SPL token rewards, or modern marketplace workflows.

## Implemented Solution

This enhancement refreshes the landing page around Gibwork as an onchain work marketplace for funded bounties, simple tasks, services, mobile submissions, and stablecoin payouts.

Changes included:

- Reworked the hero around clearer bounty/task positioning and web, iOS, and Android CTAs.
- Added proof cards for stablecoin payouts, submission tracking, and mobile readiness.
- Added a mobile bounty mockup layered over the existing dashboard visual to make the app experience visible above the fold.
- Added a product-experience section explaining the marketplace flow: post funded work, reach contributors, review submissions, and release payouts.
- Added a dedicated mobile app section for browsing bounties, submitting deliverables, and tracking wallet payouts from iOS and Android.
- Added App Store and Google Play links/badges for clearer mobile conversion.
- Updated partner/trust copy to better reflect Solana teams, open source bounties, content, QA, research, design, and service work.
- Refined requester and worker tab copy to focus on funded work, deliverables, submissions, and payouts.
- Updated FAQ content to include mobile usage and clearer payment details.
- Updated navigation, footer links, metadata, and site description for improved product accuracy.
- Hardened tweet card rendering so unavailable tweet data falls back more gracefully in the testimonial section.

## User Benefit

The refreshed page helps new visitors understand Gibwork faster:

- Teams immediately see that they can fund work and get results without a traditional hiring process.
- Contributors understand that they can discover bounties, submit work from web, iOS, or Android, track status, and get paid.
- The mobile apps now have a clear landing page presence with direct store links.
- The page aligns more closely with the current app store descriptions, live product, docs, and Gibwork marketplace positioning.
